UPTM IoT Smart Classroom Booking System (SCBS)

Bachelor's final year project, 2026

A software and hardware system that modernises campus facility management, saves energy and keeps working through power outages. It was selected to represent Universiti Poly-Tech Malaysia at ITEX 2026, the 37th International Invention, Innovation, Technology Competition & Exhibition (Malaysia, 18–19 May 2026), where the project team won a Gold Medal. A copyright was filed on 6 May 2026 and a patent application on 13 May 2026.

Book room→QR token→ESP32-CAM→Validate session→Smart lock opens
  • Responsive web app with role-based dashboards (student, lecturer and admin) and automated booking algorithms that prevent double-booked rooms, plus email reminders. It is deployed and running at ain-scbs.freedev.app.
  • QR code access replaces physical keys: an ESP32-CAM at the door validates the session and actuates the lock.
  • PIR occupancy sensors cut power to utilities after 15 minutes of vacancy.
  • Emergency power supply keeps smart locks working during outages.
  • MySQL schema and RESTful APIs, with technical documentation aligned to academic rubrics.
